Privacy Policy

AI Food Scanner – Food Health · Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nutrition AI For You ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the AI Food Scanner – Food Health app ("the app"). By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we process

  • Photos you choose to analyze. Food, menu, nutrition-label and body photos that you select or capture.
  • Profile details you enter. Name, age, gender, height, weight, goals and activity level, stored on your device.
  • Diagnostics. Crash logs, app version, device model, language and region, used to keep the app stable.

2. AI analysis of photos

Food, menu, label and body photos are sent over HTTPS to our AI processing service solely to produce the analysis you requested (for example calories, ingredients, nutrition facts or body composition estimates). Uploads are deleted after processing and in any case within 24 hours. We do not use your photos to train AI models, and we do not sell or rent them.

Body analysis requires your explicit consent before an image is uploaded. You can decline and continue using the rest of the app.

3. No facial recognition or face data

The app does not perform facial recognition, face detection, face-landmark mapping or biometric identification, and we do not collect, store or share face data. Gender used for body composition estimates is taken from the profile you fill in, not inferred from your photo. Body photos are analyzed only for body composition and are never analyzed for facial features.

4. Profile photos stay on your device

If you add an optional photo to a family profile, that photo is stored only in the app's local storage on your device. It is never uploaded to our servers or shared with third parties, and it is removed when you delete the profile or the app.

5. Advertising

The current version of the app does not show third-party advertising. If advertising is enabled in a future version, our advertising partners may process device identifiers and app-usage data to deliver and measure ads, as described in the App Store privacy label. Advertising partners never receive your food, menu or body photos.

6. Purchases

Subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed by Apple. We receive only the purchase status needed to unlock premium features. We never receive your payment card details.

7. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share data only with service providers that host and operate the AI analysis service on our behalf, and only as needed to provide the features you request. These providers are required to protect the data and may not use it for their own purposes.

8. Retention and deletion

Uploaded photos are deleted after processing and within 24 hours at most. Profile and app data remain on your device until you delete them in the app or remove the app. To request deletion of any data associated with your use of the service, contact us at the address below.

9. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal information. Contact us to exercise these rights.

11. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by a new "last updated" date on this page.
