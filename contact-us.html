Frequently asked questions

How does AI food scanning work?

Take a photo of your meal and the app's AI estimates the calories, protein, carbs, fat and fiber in seconds. You can also scan barcodes or enter food manually.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose the subscription you want to manage or cancel.

How can I delete my data?

Send us a message with the topic “Data deletion request” or email us directly, and we will process your request.

Can I suggest a new feature?

Absolutely! We'd love to receive more suggestions from our users. Choose “Feedback & suggestions” in the form above.
